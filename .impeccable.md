## Design Context

### Users
People managing persistent AI teammates, conversations, and connected app accounts on iPhone and desktop.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, calm, direct. The interface should make conversations and actions easy to find without repeating context already on screen.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing monochrome semantic tokens and bot identity colors. Use native iOS navigation, Liquid Glass, sheets and controls. On desktop, reuse the existing Base UI components. Prefer simple lists with concise profile badges over repeated cards and status labels.

### Design Principles
- Remove redundant labels, especially the conversation bot name inside its own messages.
- iOS keeps Home search and integrations within easy reach at the bottom.
- Desktop follows the Grokbot reference: notifications, search and create at the top; profile and Connect Apps at the bottom. Use restrained floating chrome, compact system typography, and an uncluttered conversation.
- Native macOS glass is one continuous window backdrop beneath readable surfaces, with opaque fallbacks for other platforms and accessibility preferences. In dark mode the sidebar/base is darker than the content plane. Define surfaces with crisp borders and rings; avoid soft shadows and white glows.
- Let the native macOS material own window blur. CSS backdrop filters over the transparent Electron renderer caused foreground text and avatar colors to smear into the backdrop; use restrained surface tints for depth instead.
- Search cached bot names immediately and request chat matches with bounded, cancellable queries.
- Give each integration one row; reveal account management and advanced tools in a drawer.
- Constrain long titles and preserve accessible names, touch targets, and larger text support.
